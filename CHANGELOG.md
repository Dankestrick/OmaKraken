# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First version.

- Bar icon with the battery on hover, and alerts at 20% and 10%.
- Lighting: on or off, Static, Breathing and Spectrum Cycling effects, brightness, speed and color, saved and sent again when the headset connects.
- Sound: sidetone on or off and its level, mic volume.
- Haptics: on or off, Low, Medium or High.
- Power: power saving on or off, 15 to 60 minutes.
- Device: battery, serial number and dongle.
- Tells you when the dongle stops answering, instead of hanging.
