# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- New look: a faint OMAKRAKEN, in the font of the Omarchy logo, repeats in
  diagonal rows behind the dropdown. It uses the theme's accent color, so it
  changes with the theme.

## 0.1.0

First version.

- Bar icon with the battery and charging state on hover, and alerts at 20% and 10% (not while charging).
- Lighting: on or off, Static, Breathing and Spectrum Cycling effects, brightness, speed and color, saved and sent again when the headset connects.
- Sound: sidetone on or off and its level, mic volume.
- Haptics: on or off, Low, Medium or High.
- Power: power saving on or off, 15 to 60 minutes.
- Device: battery and charging, serial number and dongle.
- Tells you when the dongle stops answering, instead of hanging.
